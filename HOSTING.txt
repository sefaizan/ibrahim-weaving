Ibrahim Weaving - Power Loom Ledger (installable app)

Files (keep them together, in one folder, keeping the same folder layout):
  index.html   manifest.json   service-worker.js   icons/   js/   jspdf.umd.min.js   _headers

SET UP
1. Upload the whole folder to any HTTPS static host (Netlify Drop, Cloudflare Pages,
   GitHub Pages, ...). The address must start with https:// - installing and offline use
   don't work from a local file or plain http:// (localhost is fine for testing).
2. On the phone, open that address once while online.
   Android (Chrome): menu (three dots) > Install app / Add to Home screen.
   iPhone (Safari):  Share > Add to Home Screen.
3. Open the app from its home-screen icon and leave it open a few seconds while online the
   first time, so it can save itself for offline use.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Your ledger is stored only in the phone's browser storage for that exact web address.
  The host just serves the app files; it never sees your data.
- On iPhone the home-screen app has its own storage, separate from Safari. Set up and use it
  from the home-screen icon; data entered in Safari won't appear there.
- Changing the address (new domain, renamed site) gives a fresh, empty app. Take a backup
  first and Restore it at the new address.
- Updating: replace the files on the host (Netlify: drag the folder onto the SAME site's Deploys
  tab). Before deploying, change the app-build value in the <meta name="app-build"> tag near the
  top of index.html (any new text, e.g. the date) — that is what lets phones that already have the
  app open show a "new version ready — Reload" bar. Also change CACHE_VERSION near the top of
  service-worker.js (e.g. 'v27'), since the app is now several files that phones should fetch together. The app opens the old copy once (instantly)
  while it downloads the new one; the launch after that (or tapping Reload) uses the new copy.
- Automatic email backup (Backup & Restore > Automatic email backup): once set up, the app emails a full
  backup to your own address once a day, at the "Backup time" you choose there (default 10:30 PM, so the
  backup holds the whole day's entries). If the app is open at that time it sends then; otherwise the first
  time you open it afterwards (only if something changed; never more than once per backup time, by the
  phone's own clock). Entries made after the backup time go out at the next one; use "Send a backup now"
  for anything you can't lose sooner. It goes through your own backup service (backup-service/ on
  Netlify, with Resend for the email) - the app files on the host still never see your data. The service
  address and key are typed into the app on the phone and kept in its own storage, not in these files.
  It can only send while the app is open and online. Set a password there to lock the emailed file.
  Restore one exactly like any backup: Backup & Restore > Restore from a backup > choose the file.
- Take regular backups (Backup & Restore > Share Backup). Clearing browser data or deleting
  the app erases the ledger. Backups can optionally be password-protected; a forgotten password
  cannot be recovered. The app also keeps a few automatic "safety copies" on the phone itself
  (Backup & Restore tab) - these help after a mistaken restore or edit, but they are erased
  together with the app's data, so keep sending real backups too.
- Encrypt Data (Settings): scrambles the ledger and the safety copies stored on the phone, so they can't be read
  without the PIN even if someone copies the phone's storage. Turn on PIN Lock first (with a recovery question).
  Use a 6 or 8 digit PIN - a 4 digit PIN can be guessed by a determined attacker who has a copy of the storage.
  While encryption is on the app asks for the PIN every time it is opened. Forgot the PIN: the recovery answer
  still unlocks the data. Forgot both: the data cannot be recovered except from a backup (Backup & Restore),
  so keep taking backups. Exported backups are NOT encrypted unless you tick the password option on that tab.
- Long-press the app icon (Android) for the Add Sale / Add Recovery shortcuts. They are baked
  into the APK when PWABuilder builds it, so rebuild the APK once to get them.
- Forgot the PIN: use "Forgot PIN?" on the lock screen and answer your recovery question.
  If that is forgotten too, the only way in is to clear the app's data and Restore a backup.
- The tests/ folder and package.json are for development only (run the tests with: node --test).
  You don't need to upload them to the host; the files listed at the top are all the app needs.

- Cloud Sync sign-in (Settings > Cloud Sync): sync needs an account. In the Firebase console turn on Authentication >
  Sign-in method > Email/Password, and set the Firestore rules shown in that Settings card (they already contain the owner
  address; they are the final rules - who may view or write is stored in a config/access record that only the owner can read or edit; the app creates that record, empty, the
  first time the owner signs in after the rules are pasted). Then, in the app, tap Create account, open the verification link that arrives by email, and
  tap "I've verified". Sign-in, sign-out and Forgot password are all in that same card. The phone stays signed in, including
  offline; the ledger on the phone never depends on being signed in. Signing out only stops syncing on that phone.

- View-only phones (Release 1 - email login + view-only): a phone signed in (Settings > Cloud Sync) with a verified email
  that is NOT the owner's is view-only. It shows a "View only" label in the header, hides every Add / Edit / Delete / Save /
  Restore control, swallows any tap that would change something, refuses to save, never sends anything to the cloud, and
  keeps itself up to date from the cloud copy automatically. Signing out does not unlock it (only the owner signing in on
  that phone does). The owner's phone, and a phone that never signed in to another account, behave exactly as before.
  Sign-in is always by email + password (never anonymous), and the email must be verified.

  To let someone view (all from the owner's phone - the Firebase console is not needed for this):
    1. They install the app, open Settings > Cloud Sync, tap Create account, open the verification email and tap
       "I've verified".
    2. On your phone (signed in as the owner) open Settings > People (shown only on the owner's phone). It lists everyone
       approved, with the time left on each approval and the date it ends. Type their email, choose how long ("Approved for": a number of
       minutes, hours or days - it starts at 30 days - or "until a date and time") and tap "Approve to view". You need a signal for this.
    3. On their phone they tap Sync Now. The ledger comes down and the phone shows "View only".
  In the same card, each person has three buttons: "Extend" and "Shorten" (type a number and choose minutes, hours or days; Extend
  counts from their end time, or from now if it has already ended; Shorten takes the time off and refuses if that would end it now)
  and "Revoke" (tap twice). Revoke deletes the person from the permissions record, and Firebase's rules check that record on
  every request, so they are locked out at their very next one; the app reads the record back to confirm before saying so. Their phone
  keeps the last copy it downloaded but can no longer sync. Revoking needs a signal - offline, nothing is changed and you are told.
  An approval ends at the exact moment chosen (at most 10 years ahead); approving the same email again replaces the time. Someone
  whose approval ended, or who was revoked, sees "this account isn't approved for this ledger", keeps the last copy they downloaded and stays view-only.

- Edit access on the other phone (Release 2 - time-limited, given from your phone). Approving someone with "Approve to view"
  makes them view-only. To let them EDIT, either tap "Approve to edit" instead (same email box and "Approved for" choice), or,
  for someone already on the list, tap "Allow edit" on their row (and "Stop edit" to make them view-only again). Edit access
  always ends when their approval ends - "Allow edit" does not change the end time (use Extend / Shorten for that) and is
  refused if the approval has already ended (Extend it first). "Approve to view" on someone who can already edit keeps their
  edit access and says so; use "Stop edit" to take it away. Their row reads "Can edit - 2 days left (until ...)".
  How their phone finds out: your phone keeps a small note for each person with edit access (a document in the existing "sync"
  collection whose name is a hash of their email, so viewers never see addresses; the current rules already cover it, so no rule
  change is needed - opening the People card re-sends every note). Their phone reads its note when they tap Sync Now and about
  once a minute while the app is open. While the grant is live their phone can edit and shows "Can edit - 2h 15m left" in the
  header and in Settings > Cloud Sync. It drops back to view only by itself when the end time passes, when you shorten it, revoke
  it, or use Stop edit (it notices on its next check - once a minute while the app is open, or when a push is refused), or when
  they sign out. Before it does, anything not yet synced is saved as a Safety copy of type "Unsynced entries (access ended)" in
  Settings > Backup & Restore (up to 10 are kept), and a red bar says so. Firebase itself refuses their writes the moment the
  grant ends, even if their phone is offline or its clock is wrong - the phone's screen only follows that. Note: a view-only phone
  cannot use "Restore" on that safety copy; it stays there for you or for the phone if it is given edit access again.
  Edit access on a phone is for the ledger's entries; only you (the owner) can approve, extend, revoke or give edit access, and
  only on your own phone with a signal.
  (Fallback only, if the People card is ever unavailable: the same list is the "approved" map in config/access in the Firebase
  console - a field per lowercase email holding write: false (view only) or write: true (may edit), expiresAt: a number in milliseconds since 1970, addedAt: a number.)

- Who added or changed an entry (Release 2): every entry added or changed from now on is stamped with the signed-in email and the
  time. Each row in the logs has an "Info" button (the small "i") that shows "Added by ... " and "Last edited by ... " with the
  date and time. It works on view-only phones too. Entries from before this version say "No history recorded for this entry"
  until they are edited. The stamps travel with the ledger (cloud sync and backups) and never count as an edit themselves; when
  the same entry was edited on two phones the later edit is still the one kept. A phone with no account signed in stamps the
  time only. Tip: searching a log does not look at these stamps.

- Who sees what (Release 3): every approved person has a ROLE, chosen when you approve them (Settings > People) and changeable
  from their row. A role says, for each section of the ledger (Production, Employees / looms / qualities, Sales & clients,
  Recovery & cheques, Expenses, Wages, Employee loans, Family & personal, and so on), which of four letters it allows:
  V view, A add, E edit, D delete. Add, edit and delete always include view. Two roles are built in:
    - Business viewer: Production, Sales and Cheques only (plus the lists and business name those pages need), view only.
      No family, personal, expenses, wages or loans.
    - Production operator: Production with view, add and edit (no delete); employees, looms and qualities view only;
      quantities only, no wage rates; nothing about money.
  The edit switch (Approve to edit / Allow edit / Stop edit, with an end time) still decides WHEN the add / edit / delete
  letters count: with it off, or after the time is up, every role is view only. A Business viewer has no A / E / D letters, so
  the switch changes nothing for that role. The owner (you) is never limited.
    - Changing one person: Settings > People > their row > pick a role, or tick the letters per section and press "Save access"
      (only the differences from the role are kept, so later changes to the role still reach everyone you have not adjusted).
      "Reset to role" puts them back exactly on the role.
    - Needs approval: Settings > People > their row > Sections > "Needs my approval" shows one tick box per section. Tick a
      section to say that every add, edit or delete this person makes there is to wait for your OK. It is saved with "Save access"
      (same button as the letters), kept when you change their role, end time or edit switch, and left alone by "Reset to role".
      Their row shows "Needs your approval: ..." while any are on, and their phone learns it from the same small note as the
      permissions. It never changes what they may see or do, and needs nothing in the Firebase console. You are never asked to
      approve your own changes.
    - Proposals: when a person with "Needs my approval" ticked for a section adds, edits or deletes there, the ledger is NOT changed.
      Their phone keeps a proposal instead - what they did (add / edit / delete), the section, the record, the values before and
      after, who, and when - puts the screen back as it was, and tells them it is waiting for you (their Settings > Cloud Sync
      note shows how many). An edit keeps the old and the new value; a delete is a proposal too and the record stays. One save is
      one action: if it touches a section that needs approval, everything in that save waits (a rename of a quality or employee
      changes records in several sections, and letting half of it through would leave the ledger inconsistent). A change their
      role does not allow at all is refused as before, not proposed. If a proposal cannot be kept (phone storage full, locked, or
      500 already waiting) nothing is changed and they are told. With Encrypt Data on, what a proposal holds is sealed on the phone.
      Proposals are kept on the person's phone, outside the ledger: they are not synced, not in a backup of the ledger, and you
      cannot see them on their own phone alone: each one is also SENT to you (see Approvals below).
      On their phone (Settings > Cloud Sync > "Your changes") each proposal shows "Waiting for approval"; they can Withdraw one
      (tap twice) and it is gone, nothing else changes. Once you have answered, it shows "Accepted" or "Rejected" with your note
      and can no longer be withdrawn; they can Clear it from the list.
    - Approvals (owner only, Tools > Approvals): every proposal is sent from the person's phone to the cloud (right away, or at the next
      Sync check when there is no signal; sealed with the section's key when Encrypt Data is on, never as readable text next to an
      encrypted ledger). A small \"N to approve\" badge next to the title shows how many are waiting; tap it to open the screen. Each
      entry shows who, when, the section and record, and what it was before and what it would become. Accept puts the change into
      your ledger (an edit or delete is only applied if the record is still exactly as the person saw it; otherwise it stays
      waiting and you can Reject it), Reject leaves the ledger as it is; the note box is optional and goes to the person either
      way. \"Accept all\" (tap twice) takes everything waiting from one person, oldest first; any that no longer fit stay waiting.
      Whichever phone answers first wins; a second phone is told it was already answered. The person's phone learns the answer at
      its next Sync check and shows Accepted or Rejected with your note; a person can withdraw one only while it is waiting (it is
      taken back from the cloud, which needs a connection). You need to paste the updated rules once (below) before anything is sent.
    - Your own roles: Settings > People > "New role" > name + letters per section > "Create role". You can also "Edit" a built-in
      role (an edited built-in can be reset to the original) or delete a role you made (refused while anyone has it). Saving a
      role updates everyone who has it.
    - On the other phone: only the tabs, cards and buttons the role allows are shown; Edit / Delete / Add controls the role lacks
      are hidden, a tap that slips through is stopped with a message, and saving refuses any change outside the role (last
      layer). Sections a role may not view are not kept on the phone at all. Firebase's rules enforce the same view / change
      split per section on the server.
    - Honest limits: Firebase's rules cannot tell an add from an edit from a delete inside one section document, so on the server
      they enforce "may view" and "may change (any of A / E / D)" per section; the app itself enforces the three letters
      separately. Data a person already downloaded stays on their phone after a role is narrowed or revoked.
  Approve only people you trust with what their role shows.

- Wage rates stay in Wages (Release 3): the rate history for each quality (which rate applies from which date) is kept in the
  Wages section together with the wage payments, bonuses and settlements. The Production page, the Warp beams page and the
  Settings lists of qualities, employees and looms hold names and meters only - no rate, no earning per entry. So a Production
  operator sees quality names and quantities only; the rates are never downloaded to their phone (a phone whose role cannot view
  Wages keeps none, and empties any it had from an earlier, wider role). Only the owner, or a role you give Wages, sees a rate.
  One consequence: renaming a quality or an employee in Settings also rewrites the records that use the name in other sections
  (for a quality, its rate history in Wages too). A person can do that only if their role may EDIT every section it reaches;
  otherwise the app refuses with a message, so the rates are never left behind under the old name. Rename on your own phone.

- Overview by role (Release 3): every Overview card and banner is tagged with the sections whose figures it shows, and a
  role sees it only if it may VIEW ALL of them (the tags are PERM_OVERVIEW_CARDS in js/view-only.js). Business viewer sees
  the Production, Sales and Cheques cards only: Awaiting L (AIL), Pending and Bounced Cheques, Stock Position (stock by
  quality, Produced / Sold / In Stock), Client Statement, Sales & Receivables, Receivables Aging, Clients Breakdown by
  Quality, the "Beams ending soon" banner and the Reminders banner. It does not see Cash Position, Warp Usage, Expenses &
  Material Cost, Profit / Loss or the backup reminder. The Reminders banner is checked row by row: the overdue / due-soon
  cheque rows need Recovery; the "clients quiet 30+ days" row needs Sales and Recovery; with no row allowed there is no
  banner (and dismissing it only counts the rows that person can see). The Overview page opens for a role only if at least
  one real card (not just a banner) is allowed, so a Production operator starts on Production. Cash Position needs every
  money section, so it stays on the owner's phone unless you give a role all of them. Graphs still needs every section.
  A card that is not tagged is never shown to anyone but the owner, so a card added later cannot leak by accident.

- Section keys (Release 3): with Encrypt Data on, what a person may not view is also unreadable to them. Each cloud section
  (Production, Sales, Wages, Family ...) is encrypted with its own key, and a person's phone is given only the keys for the
  sections their role may VIEW. So even if Firebase's rules were ever wrong, or someone copied a section document, a Business
  viewer cannot open Wages, Family, Expenses or Loans - there is no key for them on their phone. The older single key that sealed
  every section is no longer used for the cloud, and your PIN-locked key no longer travels with every section document; it
  lives only in a document only you can read (keys/<your email>).
    - Giving a person their keys: Settings > People > their row > Access code. The app shows a 20-character code (and Copy /
      WhatsApp buttons). Send it to them privately. On their phone they turn Encrypt Data on (Settings) if it is not on, then
      type the code once under Settings > Cloud Sync > Access code > Unlock my sections. The code is worked out from a secret on
      your phone, so you can open the same box again any time and see the same code. Their phone keeps it (sealed) to fetch
      newer keys by itself later.
    - "New code" (tap twice) on that box makes the old code stop working; they must type the new one. Use it if a code was
      seen by the wrong person.
    - Changing someone's role or sections, or approving more sections, updates their keys at once. Taking a section away,
      revoking someone, or an approval running out replaces that section's key and seals the cloud copy again with the new
      key (from the copy already in the cloud - nothing is uploaded from your phone for this, so it can never overwrite newer
      data). Keys are replaced the next time your phone is open and online: when you change access, when you open the People
      card, and at app start. They never wait on anything the other person does.
    - Honest limits: (1) what a person already downloaded stays readable on their phone - only new data is protected, exactly
      as with any revoke. (2) If Encrypt Data is off on your phone, sections are sent as plain text and only Firebase's rules
      protect them; the People card then says no code is needed. (3) A person's phone needs Encrypt Data (a PIN) on to keep
      the keys; without it the app tells them. (4) The first sync after this update re-sends every section once, sealed with
      the new keys. People who were approved before need a code (open People > their row > Access code); until they type it
      they see "enter your access code" instead of the encrypted sections. (5) "Join Encrypted Sync" (second phone of yours)
      is now owner-only; it still asks for the PIN used on your first phone.
    - Rules to paste (Settings > Cloud Sync > i, shown there in full; also pasted as-is into Firebase console > Firestore
      Database > Rules > Publish). One-time. This version's rules add: ledger/<section> (read needs V on that section, write
      needs A / E / D and the edit switch; an encrypted section can never be sent back as plain text; only you delete),
      keys/<email> (you; and each person for their own address only), and they narrow sync/ so an approved person can read
      only their own small access note (the old whole-ledger document sync/ledger is yours alone). Add / edit / delete are
      three letters on screen and in the app's save check; Firebase's rules cannot tell an edit from a delete inside one
      section document, so they enforce view (read) and change (any of A / E / D) per section.
    - Rules for Approvals (version 3.17.24): ONE new block, proposals/<id>: a person may send their own (status pending), read their own
      back, and delete their own only while still pending; only you answer (set status and note) or delete. Paste the rules from
      Settings > Cloud Sync > i again (Firebase console > Firestore Database > Rules > Publish). Until you do, proposals stay on
      the person's phone and are sent as soon as the rules are in; nothing is lost. Answered proposals stay in the cloud (you can
      delete them in the Firebase console, collection \"proposals\"); they are not counted or shown again.
    - Accepted changes (version 3.17.25): no new Firebase rule. When you Accept, the change goes through the app's normal save, so Undo, sync,
      merge and deleted-entry tracking work as for any change. The row's Info button and the Audit screen show the person who proposed it

    - Changed-since proposals (version 3.17.26): no new Firebase rule. If the record was changed after the proposal was made, Approvals shows both versions
      (what they saw, what is there now, what they want) and you choose Accept anyway (only their changed fields go on top of the record as it is
      now), Edit (your own final values) or Reject. Two proposals for one record warn each other; accepting one makes the other ask you again.
    - Pending proposals, offline and ended access (version 3.17.27): no new Firebase rule. A proposal made with no connection stays on the person's
      phone ("Not sent yet") and goes out by itself when the connection is back. When edit access ends or is revoked, nothing is deleted: proposals
      already sent stay in your Approvals inbox for you to accept or reject (the person's name is on them); ones not sent stay on their phone, marked
      "Not sent \u2014 your access has ended", and go out only if you give them access again (the rule accepts proposals made before their end time).
      Rejected proposals are never cleared from the person's list: they keep your note and what was typed. The person can still withdraw a waiting one.
    - Proposals (final checks) (version 3.17.28): no new Firebase rule and no new setting. Every action is now covered by a whole hand-over test
      between the person's phone and yours: add, edit and delete (the ledger stays as it was until you accept), accept (the change goes in through
      the normal save, stamped as their work approved by you), reject (nothing changes; they keep your note and what they typed), stale (the record
      changed since: plain Accept is refused, Accept anyway puts only their changed fields on top, a record deleted since can only be rejected),
      offline (kept as "Not sent yet", sent once when the connection is back; your own phone applies nothing without a connection) and revoked
      (already-sent ones stay in your inbox for you to answer; unsent ones stay on their phone and go out only if you give access again).
    - Approvals fixes (version 3.17.29): PASTE THE UPDATED FIREBASE RULES AGAIN (Settings > Cloud Sync > i, then Firebase console > Firestore Database >
      Rules > Publish). What changed: (1) the cloud now refuses a person's own write to a section you marked \"Needs my approval\" for them; only your
      phone writes there, when you accept; (2) audit entries and proposals need the letter that fits (a to add, e to edit, d to delete), view alone is no

YEAR REPORT (Tools > Year Report, v3.17.59)
  - Periods: the FIRST period runs from the very first entry (Dec 2025) to 31 Dec 2026 and has no opening position. From 2027 each year is
    Jan-Dec. Every period has quarter chips (Q1-Q4; the first period also has Q4 2025 and Q1-Q4 2026) and a Download PDF button.
  - Stock valuation (do it at each quarter end and on 31 Dec): date, yarn value (warp + weft together, purchase price), bills due, and the
    rate per meter for each quality. Grey cloth meters come from the app (produced less sold); only the rate is typed.
  - Opening position (on 1 Jan of each year from 2027): cash and bank, receivables per client, yarn, grey cloth (meters x rate per quality),
    employee loans per employee, bills due. The total is that year's opening capital. Fixed assets and personal loans are never included.
  - The maths: profit before drawings = sales - (business expenses incl. wages + warp + weft) + closing stock - opening stock. Drawings
    (family + personal expenses) are their own boxed line. Profit after drawings = before - drawings. Closing capital = opening capital +
    profit after drawings. The position (cash + receivables + employee loans + yarn + grey cloth - bills due) is shown with a Check line
    (position - closing capital): about 0 means everything is entered. A quarter opens with the capital the previous quarter closed with.
  - On 1 Jan 2027, after saving the opening position, the page shows the app's own position at 31 Dec 2026 next to management's figure
    and the difference. Enter a stock valuation dated 31 Dec 2026 first so that comparison is fair.
  - Without a valuation on or before a period's last day, stock counts as 0 and the page says so (so early quarters of the first period show
    low or negative profit until valuations are added). Data is saved with the Tools section; nothing to change in the Firebase console.
